ABOUT THE MANUFACTURER

Rooted in Tiruppur.
Made for the world.

LOOPER TEX is a garment manufacturing business based in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, India. Our focus includes knitwear and apparel development across infant, kids, women's and men's categories, with private-label opportunities for brands and sourcing partners.

We welcome enquiries for custom development, sampling, production capability and low-quantity programmes, subject to style, fabric and order requirements.
